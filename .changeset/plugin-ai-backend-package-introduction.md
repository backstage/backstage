Added a new `@backstage/plugin-ai-backend` package: a backend plugin that exposes the managed AI service over HTTP. Authenticated users create their own AI sessions and conversations, submit messages that stream partial and final answers as server-sent events, and read their conversation transcripts and per-session audit trails. Every route except readiness runs under the caller's user credentials and requires the `ai.session.use` permission, model and tool dispatch are authorized per attempt against the fixed `ai.model.use` and `ai.tool.use` permissions, whose granularity comes from the model and tool rules the plugin registers, and audit reads additionally require `ai.audit.read`; one user can never read or drive another user's session. An unauthenticated `GET /health` readiness endpoint reports the service's fail-open health evaluation — derived from its `ai.observability.*` events — as `200` with `status: "ok"` or `503` with `status: "degraded"`, carrying identifiers, counts, and durations only.
