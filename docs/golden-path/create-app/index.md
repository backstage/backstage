Audience: Developers and administrators

## What you will accomplish

This tutorial introduces the application generated by
[`@backstage/create-app`](../../frontend-system/building-apps/01-index.md#creating-a-new-app).
You will create a new Backstage project, run it locally, explore the included
features, and learn where its [configuration](../../conf/index.md),
[frontend system](../../frontend-system/index.md), [backend system](../../backend-system/index.md),
and example [Software Catalog](../../features/software-catalog/index.md) data live.

At the end, you will have an unchanged Backstage app running on your computer
and understand how its main pieces fit together. You will be ready to continue
with [plugin development](../plugins/index.md), [deployment](../deployment/index.md),
or an organizational [proof of concept](../adoption/003-setting-up-a-poc.md).

This tutorial does not configure [production authentication](../../auth/index.md),
connect organizational catalog data, or prepare the app for deployment. The
later Golden Paths cover those tasks.
